# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, inclusive and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected behaviour:**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
  political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or commercial solicitation —
  this project is explicitly **non-commercial**.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

- Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the repository
  maintainers via the issue tracker or the contact listed in the repository.
- Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, and may remove, edit
  or reject contributions, or ban contributors, as appropriate.
- All reports will be handled with confidentiality to the extent possible.
